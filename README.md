# Medical Robotics 2026

This is the student repository for **Medical Robotics (Robótica Médica)** at the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon (**FCUL**). It contains the instructions, notebooks, code, and supporting files for the course's practical work. New material will be published progressively during the semester.

Medical robotics lies at the intersection of computing, engineering, and medicine. The course examines how robotic systems are used in surgery, image-guided intervention, rehabilitation, and patient assistance. It combines robotics fundamentals with the technical, clinical, ethical, and societal questions raised by medical applications.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- explain the principal medical applications of robotic systems;
- apply robotics methods including kinematics, control, and visual servoing;
- analyse the capabilities and limitations of surgical robots;
- select suitable robotic approaches for different medical applications;
- design and implement assistive-robotics solutions with patient needs in mind; and
- discuss the ethical and societal implications of autonomous medical systems.

## Using this repository

Begin with [Week 1 — Environment Setup](week-1/README.md). Each group maintains one shared repository for the semester and submits its work to Moodle as a Git bundle. The collaboration, update, and submission workflow is explained in the [Git introduction](week-1/git-intro.md).

Keep the code, notebooks, and report material requested for each session inside its corresponding `week-N/` folder.

## Getting help

For installation problems, consult the troubleshooting section in the [Week 1 guide](week-1/README.md#troubleshooting). If the problem remains, contact the instructor with the exact error message or a screenshot and indicate your operating system.
